EarGuard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Summary in one line: EarGuard does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

1. Who we are

EarGuard is an educational mobile application that helps users understand how loud their earphones and headphones may be, based on public device specifications and World Health Organization (WHO) safe-listening guidance.

Contact: kimsunseong51@gmail.com

2. What data we collect

We collect no personal data. Specifically:

  • No name, email address, phone number, or account is required to use EarGuard.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising, or profiling SDKs are used.
  • No location, contacts, calendar, camera, photos, SMS, or call logs are accessed.
  • We do not use cookies or third-party trackers.
  • We do not access your microphone.

3. Data stored on your device

EarGuard stores the following information locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to us or anyone else:

  • Your listening session history (start time, end time, volume level, breaks taken)
  • Your app preferences (e.g., notification toggle state)

This data can be deleted at any time from Settings → "Clear my data".

4. Permissions

EarGuard requests only one permission:

  • Notifications — requested only when you tap "Start listening session" or toggle "Break reminders" on. Used solely to send you local break reminders. You can deny this and still use the app fully.

EarGuard does not request: Location, Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Storage / Files, SMS or phone.

5. Network activity

EarGuard does not require an internet connection to function. The app may open external links (e.g., to WHO resources or Amazon product searches) in your browser when you tap them. Those third-party sites have their own privacy policies.

6. Children's privacy

EarGuard is rated 13+. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Your rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to delete, export, or modify on our servers. All app data is local to your device and can be cleared from within the app at any time.

8. Data sources

EarGuard uses publicly available information from the World Health Organization (Make Listening Safe, WHO-ITU H.870), AutoEQ, Crinacle, RTINGS, and manufacturer-published specifications.

9. Medical disclaimer

EarGuard is an educational tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. All estimates are based on public specifications and are for informational purposes only. For hearing concerns, consult a qualified audiologist or doctor.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
